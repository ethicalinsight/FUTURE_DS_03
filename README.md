  # Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analytics

An end-to-end growth analytics project analyzing marketing funnel data in Power BI to identify conversion drop-offs, evaluate channel performance, and optimize lead-to-customer conversion efficiency.

---

## 📌 Task Overview & Objective
* **Objective:** Analyze marketing funnel metrics across customer acquisition stages to pinpoint significant drop-off points, evaluate marketing channel performance, and deliver actionable strategy recommendations to improve conversion rates.
* **Track:** Data Science / Business Intelligence
* **Primary Deliverable:** Interactive Power BI funnel dashboard supported by an executive insight report.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Data Processing & Cleaning:** Microsoft Excel
* **Data Visualization & Analytics:** Power BI (DAX, Funnel Visuals, Interactive Dashboards)
* **Data Source:** Kaggle Marketing Funnel Dataset

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Acquisition & Import:** Extracted raw marketing funnel dataset from Kaggle into Excel.
2. **Data Preprocessing (Excel):** Handled missing records, standardized field formats, and structured categorical attributes for relational modeling.
3. **Dashboard Development (Power BI):** 
   * Constructed DAX measures for stage-by-stage conversion KPIs and drop-off percentages.
   * Designed intuitive funnel visuals highlighting lead progression through the pipeline.
   * Built comparative visual metrics to evaluate conversion efficiency across individual marketing channels.
4. **Insight Generation & Reporting:** Synthesized findings into executive recommendations targeting bottleneck reduction.

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Business Value
* **Drop-Off Identification:** Isolated the specific funnel stages experiencing the highest customer attrition.
* **Channel Efficiency:** Benchmark performance metrics across channels to determine optimal marketing ROI.
* **Funnel Optimization:** Formulated data-driven recommendations to improve top-of-funnel conversion velocity and overall lead-to-customer conversion.
